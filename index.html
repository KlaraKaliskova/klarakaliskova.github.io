Klára Kalíšková

I am an Associate Professor at the Faculty of Informatics and Statistics, Prague University of Economics and Business, a researcher at the IDEA think tank, and an Associated Scientist at the Economics Institute of the Czech Academy of Sciences. I have a PhD from CERGE-EI, Prague, and my main research interests lie in empirical and policy-oriented research in the fields of labor economics and applied econometrics.
